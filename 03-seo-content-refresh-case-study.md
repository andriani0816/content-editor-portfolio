# SEO Content Refresh Case Study: Improving an Underperforming Gaming Article

**Portfolio type:** Independent Content Portfolio Project  
**Content type:** SEO content optimisation case study  
**Target audience:** Gaming and digital marketplace readers  
**Primary skill demonstrated:** Content refresh and SEO optimization  
**Objective:** Improve search visibility, usefulness, engagement, and commercial performance

## Case Study Objective

This project demonstrates how I would evaluate and refresh an existing gaming article that is no longer performing effectively. The approach combines search-intent analysis, content-quality review, fact-checking, on-page SEO, reader experience, internal linking, and performance measurement.

Rather than simply rewriting the article, the goal is to identify why the existing content may be underperforming and make targeted improvements that can be evaluated through measurable KPIs.

## Scenario

Assume an existing article titled:

**"How to Choose the Right PC Game for You"**

has experienced declining organic traffic and engagement.

The article still receives impressions in search results, but its click-through rate, average ranking position, and product-page engagement have weakened.

The task is to diagnose potential content problems and develop a structured refresh strategy.

## 1. Content Audit
Before making changes, I would audit the existing article to understand why its performance may have declined. The review would combine search-performance data, editorial analysis, search intent, content freshness, and commercial relevance.

### Potential Problems Identified

**1. Search intent may not be sufficiently clear**

The existing article may provide general information about choosing PC games without adequately supporting readers who are actively comparing options before purchase.

The refreshed content should therefore better address questions such as:

- What type of PC game fits the reader's preferences?
- What hardware requirements should be checked?
- How should price and available editions be compared?
- What should buyers verify before purchasing?
- How can readers narrow down their choices efficiently?

**2. The title may be too generic**

The title "How to Choose the Right PC Game for You" explains the topic but provides limited detail about the value readers will receive.

A stronger title should communicate both the topic and the practical decision the article helps the reader make.

**3. The introduction may not answer the reader's need quickly enough**

Users arriving from search should immediately understand what the article will help them accomplish.

The refreshed introduction should therefore establish the decision problem quickly and explain how the guide will help.

**4. Content structure may need improvement**

Long paragraphs and broad sections can make comparison-oriented content difficult to scan.

The refreshed version should use:

- Descriptive headings
- Shorter paragraphs
- Bullet points
- Decision-support sections
- Clear comparison criteria
- A concise final recommendation framework

**5. Information may require freshness checks**

Gaming information can change over time. Check product availability, platform requirements, pricing, editions, and other purchase-related details before updating the article.

Validate any time-sensitive information against current authoritative sources.

**6. Commercial pathways may be weak**

An informational article can attract traffic without effectively helping readers take the next step.

Relevant internal links can connect readers to game categories, product pages, edition comparisons, deals, or related buying guides when they genuinely support the user's decision.

## 2. Search Intent and Keyword Strategy

The primary search intent is a combination of **informational** and **commercial investigation**.

The reader is not necessarily ready to purchase immediately. They may still be deciding what type of game fits their preferences, hardware, and budget.

### Primary Topic

**How to choose a PC game**

### Potential Supporting Search Topics

- best PC game for me
- how to choose PC games
- PC game buying guide
- how to check PC game requirements
- Standard vs Deluxe game editions
- things to check before buying PC games

Keywords should be incorporated naturally. The priority is satisfying the reader's intent rather than repeating search terms unnecessarily.

## 3. Proposed SEO Refresh

### Original Title

**How to Choose the Right PC Game for You**

### Proposed Updated Title

**How to Choose a PC Game: 7 Things to Check Before You Buy**

### Proposed Meta Description

Not sure which PC game to buy? Learn what to check before purchasing, including genre, system requirements, platform, editions, price, and replay value.

### Proposed Content Structure

1. Start With the Genres You Actually Enjoy
2. Check System Requirements Before Buying
3. Confirm Platform and Activation Requirements
4. Compare Standard, Deluxe, and Other Editions
5. Consider Price Against Included Content
6. Check Multiplayer and Online Requirements
7. Think About Replay Value and Your Gaming Habits

This structure makes the article easier to scan while aligning each section with a specific purchase consideration.

## 4. Example: Before and After Optimisation

### Before

> Choosing a PC game can be difficult because many games are available. Genres and prices vary, so players should research games before buying.

### After

> Thousands of PC games compete for your attention, but the best choice is not necessarily the newest release or the biggest discount. Before buying, check whether the game matches your preferred genre, PC specifications, platform, budget, and playing habits. A few minutes of comparison can help you avoid paying for a game—or a premium edition—that does not fit the way you actually play.

### Why the Updated Version Is Stronger

The revised introduction:

- Identifies the reader's decision problem immediately.
- Introduces concrete purchase criteria.
- Creates a clearer reason to continue reading.
- Connects informational guidance with commercial decision-making.
- Uses natural language rather than keyword repetition.

## 5. Internal Linking Strategy

The refreshed article could link naturally to:

- Digital game buying guides
- Standard vs Deluxe edition comparisons
- Relevant PC game categories
- Current deals or price-comparison pages
- Individual product pages where appropriate

Internal links should be contextually relevant and help readers progress through their decision journey.

## 6. Fact-Checking and Content Freshness

Before republishing the refreshed article, I would verify:

- System requirements
- Platform compatibility
- Activation requirements
- Edition contents
- Regional restrictions where relevant
- Current product availability
- Time-sensitive pricing or promotional claims

Check important factual claims against authoritative, current sources. Review information likely to change during future content refreshes.

## 7. Performance Measurement

Because this is an independent portfolio case study rather than a live website experiment, I don't claim real performance improvement here.

If the refreshed article were published, I would compare pre-refresh and post-refresh performance using metrics such as:

| KPI | What I Would Evaluate |
| --- | --- |
| Organic impressions | Whether search visibility increases |
| Average ranking position | Whether relevant queries improve in search |
| Organic CTR | Whether the revised title and search presentation attract more clicks |
| Organic clicks | Whether search traffic increases |
| Engagement | Whether readers interact more meaningfully with the content |
| Internal-link CTR | Whether readers continue to relevant comparison or product pages |
| Commercial click-through | Whether the article supports movement toward product discovery |
| Conversion-related actions | Whether commercially relevant user actions improve |

Assess performance over a meaningful comparison period rather than automatically attributing short-term fluctuations to the content refresh.

## 8. Iteration Plan

Content optimisation should continue after publication.

If impressions increased but CTR remained weak, I would reassess the title and search-result positioning.

If traffic increased but engagement remained weak, I would review whether the article satisfies the search intent quickly enough.

If readers engaged with the article but rarely continued to relevant product pages, I would evaluate internal-link placement, CTA relevance, and the connection between informational and commercial content.

The goal is not simply to publish an updated article, but to use performance evidence to guide the next editorial decision.

## Case Study Takeaway

This case study demonstrates a structured approach to content optimisation: diagnose the problem, understand search intent, improve the content, verify factual information, connect readers to useful next steps, measure performance, and iterate based on evidence.

A successful content refresh should improve the reader experience while supporting measurable search and commercial objectives.
